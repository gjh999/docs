QBop — Privacy Policy

App package (Android): kr.co.jennysoft.qbop
App Store ID (iOS): 6788213802
Effective: May 15, 2026
Last updated: July 7, 2026

Jennysoft ("the Company") provides the mobile application QBop ("the App", available on Android and iOS) and values your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed while you use the App, and its purpose, retention, and any sharing with third parties. The App requires no sign-up or login, and the Company does not store your personal information on any account or backend server of its own.

1. Information We Process

The App can be used without registration. The following information may be processed.

CategoryItemsPurpose
Location Device GPS/network location (latitude, longitude), reverse-geocoded address string Search nearby businesses, show distance, launch map/navigation apps, remember last search location
App settings Language, category/sort/search radius, navigation & map-app choice, excluded place IDs, ad-free status, etc. Provide features and persist your settings (all stored on-device)
In-app purchases App Store / Google Play purchase identifiers (e.g., purchase tokens), ad-free flag Process "Buy me a coffee" support, apply "Remove Ads", restore purchases
Advertising Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, etc.), device & app info (per Google AdMob policy) Show banner ads (hidden once ads are removed via support purchase)
Technical App version, OS info, network error logs (as needed) Service stability and troubleshooting

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information. You may grant, deny, or revoke the location permission in your device (Android/iOS) settings; if denied, location-based features such as nearby search are limited.

2. Where Information Is Processed (Third-Party Services)

  • On device: App settings, last search coordinates, ad-free status, etc. are stored only on your device and are not sent to the Company's servers.
  • Kakao: To search nearby businesses and fetch details, the App calls the Kakao Local API; your query and coordinates are sent to Kakao. Kakao's privacy policy applies.
    • Kakao Privacy Policy
  • Google: Advertising (AdMob) and Google Play in-app purchases. Google's privacy policy applies.
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Apple: App Store in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies.
    • Apple Privacy Policy

3. Retention

  • Locally stored data: removed when the App is uninstalled or when you clear the App's data.
  • In-app purchase records: retained by App Store / Google Play for periods required by those stores and applicable law, and as needed for dispute/refund handling.

4. Sharing and Processing by Third Parties

The Company does not sell your personal information. To provide the service, it uses the third-party services below (including overseas providers), and information may be transmitted to and processed by them.

  • Access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information (including clearing App data)
  • Location & advertising identifiers: reset permissions/advertising ID in device (Android/iOS) settings; deny "App Tracking" requests on iOS
  • In-app purchases: subject to App Store / Google Play order history and refund policies

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we may cease processing information for users identified as under that age.
