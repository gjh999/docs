QBap Privacy Policy

App package: kr.co.jennysoft.qbop
Effective date: May 15, 2026
Last revised: May 17, 2026

Jenisoft (hereinafter “the Company”) provides the mobile application QBap (Korean name: 큐밥, hereinafter “the App”) and values your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, and for what purposes, how long it is retained, and any disclosure or processing entrusted to third parties.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The App can be used without account registration. The following information may be processed.

Category Items Purpose
Location Device GPS or network-based location (latitude/longitude), reverse-geocoded address text Nearby restaurant search, distance display, map and navigation app integration, saving last search location (convenience on relaunch)
App settings Language, sort/filter/search radius, navigation mode and map app choice, excluded place IDs, etc. Providing App features and preserving settings (stored on device)
In-app purchases Google Play payment and purchase identifiers (e.g. purchase token), ad-removal status Processing “Buy us a coffee” support payments, applying ad removal, purchase restore and server verification (when configured)
Advertising Advertising ID, device and app information, etc. (per Google AdMob policies) Displaying banner ads (not shown when ads are removed via support)
Push notifications FCM token, receive on/off (on device), alert title and body Broadcast push (can be disabled in Settings; Firebase Cloud Messaging)
Technical data App version, OS information, network error logs, etc. (when needed) Service stability and error response

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. You may allow, deny, or revoke location permission in Android settings; if denied, location-based features such as nearby search are limited.

2. Where Information Is Processed

  • On your device: App settings, last search coordinates, ad-removal status, purchase token (local), etc. may be stored on your device.
  • Google: Places API (nearby places and details), Google Play billing, AdMob, etc. — Google’s Privacy Policy applies.
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • Google advertising technologies
  • Firebase: Push notifications (FCM) — Google/Firebase policies apply.
  • Company-operated server (optional): When support payment verification or reference price APIs are used, purchase tokens, access IP (for country estimation), etc. may be sent over HTTPS. The server URL depends on the App build configuration.
  • Exchange rates: For reference price display, the server may call a public exchange-rate API (Frankfurter); no personally identifiable information is sent.

3. Retention Period

  • Locally stored App data: removed when you uninstall the App, or until you clear App data.
  • In-app purchases and verification: may be retained per Google Play and applicable law, and as needed for disputes or refunds.
  • Purchase verification records on the server (when operated): destroyed without delay after the verification purpose is achieved; retained only as required by law.

4. Third-Party Disclosure and Processing Entrustment

The Company does not sell your personal information to third parties in principle. To provide the service, we may use the following third parties (including overseas providers), and information may be transmitted to and processed by them.

Recipient / processor Items provided / processed Purpose
Google LLC and affiliates Location and place search requests, advertising ID, payment-related information Places and maps integration, Play billing, AdMob
API server operated by the Company (when applicable) Purchase token, access IP Support payment verification, reference price

5. Your Rights

You may request the following; the Company will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

  • Access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of personal information
  • Location and advertising ID: manage permissions and reset advertising ID in Android device settings
  • In-app purchases: per Google Play order history and refund policies

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14. If we learn that a user is under that age, we may take steps to stop processing related information.
